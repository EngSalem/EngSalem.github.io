AI-Assisted Reviews Should Be Argumentative
July 2026 Position ~9 min read

A case for review assistants that evaluate the human reviewer's reasoning rather than replacing it. Instead of writing reviews, an assistant should decompose a human review into its argument roles — facts, evaluations, requests — and return role-specific feedback, keeping scientific judgment where it belongs.
